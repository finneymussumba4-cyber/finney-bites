About FINNEY BITES

Fresh Food, Fast Delivery!

Welcome to FINNEY BITES, your go-to food destination around Zetech University in Kiambu County. We are passionate about making delicious meals accessible and convenient.

What We Offer

From tasty burgers and crispy chicken to freshly prepared pizzas, chips, snacks and refreshing drinks, our menu offers something for every craving.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide delicious food, convenient ordering, friendly customer service and reliable delivery. You can order for delivery or collect your food through pickup.

Delivery and Convenience

Enjoy free delivery on orders worth KSh 2,000 and above. We serve customers around Zetech University and nearby areas.
